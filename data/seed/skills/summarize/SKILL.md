# Summarize

Use when condensing long or dense content.

1. **Read for the spine.** Identify the thesis, the key claims, and the
   conclusion before writing anything.
2. **Be faithful.** Never introduce facts not in the source; preserve important
   caveats and numbers exactly.
3. **Structure for the reader.** Lead with a one-line takeaway, then the few
   points that actually matter. Drop repetition and filler.
4. **Match the length to the need.** A summary that's almost as long as the
   source isn't a summary — be selective about what you keep.
