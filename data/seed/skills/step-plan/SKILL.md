# Step Planner

Use before tackling a multi-step task.

1. **Restate the goal** in one sentence, including the definition of "done".
2. **Decompose** into the smallest ordered steps that each produce a checkable
   result. Note dependencies between steps.
3. **Identify risks** and the unknowns that could invalidate the plan; decide
   what to verify first.
4. **Act and check.** Execute a step, verify its result against the goal, then
   continue. Revise the plan when reality disagrees — don't push a broken plan.
