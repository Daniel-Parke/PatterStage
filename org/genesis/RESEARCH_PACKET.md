# RP-NN · name the workstream

One packet per material workstream. Copy this form to
`docs/research/RP-NN.md`, once per workstream. A packet carries sourced
facts and the decisions they settle, and nothing else. No background, no
tutorial. A packet that only restates what any competent reader already
knows is a defect: delete it and note that the workstream needed no
research.

Until Genesis runs this file is a blank form.

## What this packet decides

The decisions waiting on this research, by name. Each one names the
product map section or the work package that is blocked without it. If
nothing is blocked, stop here and do not write the packet.

## Stopping condition

Research stops when every decision listed above can be made. Write the
condition out in one line so a reader can tell whether it has been met.

## Cap

150 lines. Reaching the cap without meeting the stopping condition is a
finding: say what is still open and hand it to the operator rather than
carrying on.

## Sourced facts

One row per fact. A fact with no source is an opinion and goes in the
next section instead.

| fact | source and date | version or edition | terms of use | what it decides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Judgement

What the facts do not settle, and the reading taken anyway. Label each
one as a judgement, not a fact, and say what would change it.

## Decisions settled

| decision | the ruling | the facts it rests on | where it is recorded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Changed nothing

If this research changed no decision, say so here in one line and close
the packet. That line is the whole output and writing it is the correct
result, not a failure.

## Conflicts

Where this packet contradicts another packet, the product map, the
lock-book or an adopted pack. Name the other artefact and the exact
disagreement. A contradiction found here is cheap; the same one found
during integration is not.

## Still open

Questions this packet did not answer, and who now owns each one.
