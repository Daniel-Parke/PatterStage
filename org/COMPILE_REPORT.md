tooling once records exist; they are never seed files and never
hand-edited", and check `E011` compares them against their generator. So
they were generated on 2026-08-22 from the twenty eight records, and both
round-trip against `E011` exactly.

Generating them cost four errors, taking the run from 45 to 48:

- `D001 org/TASKS.md: missing compiled_from`
- `D001 org/STATE.md: missing compiled_from`
- `D003 org/TASKS.md: not required at scale ORG, not an add-on, not
  marked authored in the compile report`
- `D003 org/STATE.md: not required at scale ORG, not an add-on, not
  marked authored in the compile report`

**Neither is fixable from this repository, and both were attempted.**
The generator writes `derived: true` into the front-matter and
deliberately writes no `compiled_from`, because a generated file has no
template ancestry to name. Adding `compiled_from` by hand would satisfy
`D001` and would immediately fail `E011`, which compares the file
byte-for-byte against what the generator would write: the two checks
demand opposite bytes. `D003` accepts an ancestry row whose source word
is `authored`, `normalised` or `preserved`, and a generated view is none
of the three; the rows added above say `derived view` truthfully and
therefore do not clear the check. The alternative, deleting the two
files, restores 44 errors and puts five pointers in the seed back to
nothing, which is the defect this repair was opened to fix.

So the choice was between a seed that is honest and a seed that is two
errors quieter, and honest won. Both errors are filed as friction in
`docs/EOS_FEEDBACK.md`, dated 2026-08-22, with the one-line fix: have
`D001` and `D003` skip any file whose front-matter carries `derived:
true`. This deviation ends the day that lands, and it needs no change
here.
