## Independent verdict, 28 September 2026

The read-only sceptic first rejected the draft because 91 split dispositions
had no individual owner, several batch claims omitted live files, Q-011 had
no prerelease Hindsight, Story Weaver or legacy-warning work, five runnable
proofs were deferred, and numeric goals appeared to be savings forecasts.
The coordinator added 163 split rows, explicit claims and prerelease batches,
reassigned 33 runnable proofs, labelled targets as goals, and added the final
disposition contract.

The second pass rejected four specific ruling mismatches: tooling-11b proposed
a Compose volume rename despite the keep ruling; org-11 and tooling-12b
disagreed about the EOS compiler; tooling-10b and critic-05b had wrong timing;
and app-01a-d/h plus tooling-02 lacked the API-label, caller-gate and npm
command claims. The coordinator corrected those rows and named the specific
T-0187 test-identity exception.

The third pass found missing alias-reader claims and the rest of the
lib-data-05 removal path. T-0200 now claims all 62 current tracked source and
script alias-use files; its plan oracle recomputes that inventory. T-0199
claims the database caller, shell warning, documentation, integration test and
preserved backup-listing tests. The final targeted pass found and then
confirmed correction of one accountable-owner mismatch: lib-data-05 and its
split disposition both point to T-0199.

**Verdict:** ready for separate operator approval on the reviewed plan scope.
The sceptic made no edits. The coordinator's focused plan and closing-oracle
suites passed 6/6 after the final ownership correction; full gate, mutation
sweep and hosted CI are separate T-0165 landing evidence.
