## Source-level limits and next probe

The source checks establish reachable patterns and call sites, not the prevalence of legacy data or duplicates in user databases. In particular, `lib-data-08` is not a licence to drop benchmark columns or tables, and `lib-data-10b` does not establish that any specific install has duplicate rows. `lib-data-05` cannot establish a real install's version; `lib-data-09` and `lib-domains-05` cannot exclude external or dynamic readers. Similar-looking helpers in `lib-data-14`, `lib-data-18` and `lib-domains-11` have unmeasured interface cost or different semantics. No fixed verdict is claimed without an implementation and behaviour check. The next disposable SQLite probe is `lib-data-27`: compare `sqlite_master` after the real v2-to-v43 path with a fresh path, specifically `idx_runs_story`.
