# Trademark Policy

The software in this repository is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). That license lets you use, modify, and redistribute the code. It does **not** let you use our names or visual branding as your own product identity. Apache 2.0 Section 6 reserves all trademark rights to the licensor.

## Protected names

The following are reserved and may not be used in a way that suggests affiliation, endorsement, or an official product without permission:

- **Patter**
- **PatterStage**
- **PatterTech**
- **PatterOS** and the other repositories in the Patter family
- Any name following the pattern **Patter\*** in a project, product, distribution, domain, or marketing name

## Protected visuals

The following are reserved for official PatterStage and PatterTech use:

- Logos, icons, and brand assets in [`branding/assets/`](branding/assets/)
- The Cherenkov colour palette and related visual identity described in [`branding/guidelines/COLORS.md`](branding/guidelines/COLORS.md) — including the neon-glow treatment used as part of the Patter look. The canonical implementation lives in [`docs/contributing/design-tokens.md`](docs/contributing/design-tokens.md), [`src/lib/theme.ts`](src/lib/theme.ts), and [`src/app/globals.css`](src/app/globals.css).

Files under [`branding/`](branding/) are **not** licensed under Apache 2.0. See [`branding/LICENSE.md`](branding/LICENSE.md).

## What you can do without asking

- Say your project is *based on* or *compatible with* PatterStage, with a clear statement that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by PatterTech
- Fork the project and do whatever you want, as long as you **remove all branding**
- Refer to PatterStage or PatterTech in factual, non-misleading documentation

## What you cannot do

- Name your fork or distribution anything containing **Patter\***
- Imply that your modified version is official, endorsed, or maintained by PatterTech
- Reuse our logos, icons, or the Cherenkov colour theme as your product's visual identity
- Redistribute files from [`branding/`](branding/) as part of your own product

## Forks and derivatives

If you distribute a modified version of this software, you must choose your own name and visual identity. See [REBRANDING.md](REBRANDING.md) for a short checklist.
