### Refactor 1 — `setErrorFromCaught` helper promotion

Added `setErrorFromCaught(setError, err, fallback)` to `src/lib/api-fetch.ts` as the `setError` sibling of the existing `toastError(showToast, err, fallback)` helper. Same composition rules (toError() + `|| fallback` discipline), different consumer. The new helper is the canonical replacement for the 9 sites that did `setError(messageFromError(err, "..."))` — one fewer intermediate variable and one less line in the common catch block.

Byte-equivalence to the inline form is documented in the JSDoc and proven by the byte-equivalence test in `tests/unit/set-error-from-caught.test.ts`:
```ts
directSetError(messageFromError(err, fallback));
setErrorFromCaught(helperSetError, err, fallback);
expect(helperCalls).toEqual(directCalls);
```
Both code paths produce the same `setError` call for 6 different caught-value shapes (Error, empty Error, string, null, undefined, TypeError).
